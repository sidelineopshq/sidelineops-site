About SidelineOps

One place for the details that make a season work

SidelineOps HQ is based in Madison, Alabama and founded by Scott South. The product was created to solve the weekly operational friction coaches and program leaders know well: moving schedules, scattered parent updates, volunteer coverage, and game-day details that live in too many places.

Scott has been involved in youth sports for more than 14 years through coaching, serving as a youth program board member, and working as a booster club officer. That background gives him a practical view of the needs of coaches, parents, volunteers, booster leaders, and school administrators involved in a program.

SidelineOps HQ is intentionally early and transparent. The product is currently in beta with real high-school athletic programs, including James Clemens Softball, so coach and program feedback can shape what gets built before broader release.
