A quick guide for parents and guardians

Your team's schedule and updates, in one dependable place.

If your athletic program uses SidelineOps HQ, your coach can share schedules, last-minute changes, team details, and volunteer needs without asking you to keep up with another app.

What to expect

The information parents need most

Schedule

See the current plan

  • View your program's public schedule without logging in
  • See dates, times, locations, and team details
  • Add the schedule to your personal calendar
Updates

Know when plans change

  • Receive automated notices when your program updates an event
  • Get the details your coach chooses to share by email or GroupMe
  • Choose optional SMS alerts when your program has them enabled
Volunteers

Help when you can

  • See open volunteer needs for games and events
  • Sign up for available roles from a shared link
  • Receive reminders for your assignments
Getting connected

Your coach or program will send what you need

Watch for a link or QR code from your program. From there, you may be asked to select your player, provide contact information, and choose the notifications you want to receive.

Simple access

No new parent subscription

No app download is required

You can view the public schedule from your phone or browser. Parents do not pay a SidelineOps subscription fee.
Your choices

You control optional text alerts

SMS consent is separate and optional

You can still participate in your program and receive available non-SMS communication without agreeing to text messages.

Start with the link from your program

Your team's schedule and signup page are specific to your school and sport. Use the link or QR code shared by your coach, booster club, or program leader.
