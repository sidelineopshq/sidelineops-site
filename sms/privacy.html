SidelineOps HQ SMS Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Company and messaging brand

SidelineOps HQ, LLC is the company and messaging brand for the SidelineOps HQ SMS program.

Mobile information is not used for marketing

SidelineOps HQ does not share, sell, rent, transfer, or provide mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in data, text messaging opt-in data, or messaging consent to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent are excluded from all data-sharing arrangements.

Information collected

When a parent or guardian voluntarily opts in, SidelineOps HQ collects the mobile phone number, the date and time of consent, the source and version of the consent disclosure, consent evidence, and the athletic program associated with the request.

How mobile information is used

Mobile information is used only to provide requested operational athletic-program alerts, including schedule changes, event cancellations, and closely related team logistics. SidelineOps HQ does not send promotional or third-party marketing messages through this program.

Messaging service providers

Contracted messaging providers process mobile information only on SidelineOps HQ's behalf to deliver and support messages requested by the user. They are not permitted to use mobile information, opt-in data, or messaging consent for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

Consent, frequency, and choice

SMS consent is optional, is collected through a separate checkbox that is unchecked by default, and is not a condition of signup, purchase, participation, or service. Message frequency varies based on athletic schedule activity. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

Questions

Contact support@sidelineopshq.com with privacy or messaging questions.
